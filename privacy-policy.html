Privacy Policy for Stampiary

Effective Date: 29.07.2026
Last Updated: 29.07.2026

Introduction

Stampiary ("we," "our," or "us") is a geography and culture exploration app that lets users discover facts, take quizzes, and collect stamps for countries around the world. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the Stampiary mobile application (the "App").

We designed Stampiary to work offline-first and to minimize the personal information we collect. Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not access or use the App.

Information We Collect

Information We Do NOT Collect

Stampiary does not require account creation, login, or sign-up. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information directly through app functionality.

Locally Stored Information

The App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Your progress (which countries you have visited, quizzes completed, matching games played)
  • Streak and Daily Flight completion history
  • Personal notes you optionally add to stamps in your passport
  • Selected mode (Kid or General)

This locally stored information is not transmitted to us and remains on your device unless you choose to share it (for example, through the Passport Share feature, which generates a shareable image containing only your stamp count and streak number, not your personal notes).

Automatically Collected Information (via Third-Party Services)

We use third-party services that may automatically collect certain technical information, as described in the "Third-Party Services" section below.

How We Use Information

Since we collect minimal information directly, our use is limited to:

  • Downloading and caching country content (facts, quizzes, images) from our content delivery service so the App can function offline
  • Displaying advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Verifying that requests to our backend come from a genuine, unmodified copy of the App (via Firebase App Check), to protect against abuse

Third-Party Services

Stampiary uses the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data:

Google Firebase

We use Firebase Storage to host and deliver country content, and Firebase App Check to verify app authenticity and prevent abuse. Firebase may process technical data such as IP address and device identifiers as part of these services.
Firebase Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the App. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and general usage data to serve ads. Depending on your selected mode (see "Children's Privacy" below), AdMob will serve either standard or non-personalized advertising.
Google AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
You can review and adjust your ad personalization settings at: https://adssettings.google.com

Google Play Services

As an Android application, Stampiary relies on Google Play Services, which has its own data collection practices.
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

Stampiary offers a Kid Mode designed for younger users, alongside a General Mode for older users and adults. We take children's privacy seriously and have designed the App to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy requirements:

  • The App does not require any account creation or collection of personal information from any user, including children.
  • When Kid Mode is active, the App requests only non-personalized advertising from AdMob (using tagForChildDirectedTreatment and tagForUnderAgeOfConsent signals), meaning no behavioral advertising profile is built for child users.
  • Switching from Kid Mode to General Mode requires completing a simple parental gate (a basic math question) to help ensure the switch is made by an adult.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the information below so we can address it.

Advertising

Stampiary is supported by advertising displayed through Google AdMob. Ad placements include:

  • A banner advertisement on the home screen
  • An interstitial (full-screen) advertisement shown after completing a country quiz in General Mode's deep-dive quiz flow

The Daily Flight feature is always ad-free. We limit how frequently interstitial ads can appear to avoid excessive interruption.

Data Security

We rely on industry-standard security practices provided by our third-party service providers (Google Firebase, Google AdMob) to protect data in transit. Since most user data remains locally on your device, your device's own security settings (passcode, encryption) are an important part of protecting your information.

Data Retention

Locally stored progress data remains on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data through your device settings. We do not maintain a server-side copy of your personal progress, notes, or usage history.

Your Rights and Choices

  • Ad personalization: You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings (Android Settings > Google > Ads) or at https://adssettings.google.com
  • Local data: You can delete all locally stored progress at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data/cache through your device settings.
  • Do Not Track: Because Stampiary does not track users across other apps or websites for advertising purposes beyond what AdMob independently performs, "Do Not Track" browser signals are not applicable to app usage.

International Users

If you are using Stampiary from outside the country where our services are hosted, please be aware that information handled by our third-party service providers (Firebase, AdMob) may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States or other countries where their servers are located.
